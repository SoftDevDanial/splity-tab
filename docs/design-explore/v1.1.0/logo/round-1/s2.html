S2 · The receipt S

One paper strip curls into an S, its loose end torn with a short serrated edge, so the receipt itself writes the letter.

Construction

One strip 8 units wide on the 48 grid, one width through every run and bend. Bends on centre radii of 6.5 and 7, the lower a touch larger, joined by a straight spine at 30°. Nothing folds or crosses. Every corner is a sharp cut.

Pressure tests

Dollar sign. Nothing vertical, nothing crosses the spine or passes the top and bottom edges, and the tear points sideways.

Snake. One width end to end, no taper, no head. A square cut sits where a head would be.

Scroll. Watched hardest. No end rolls, no back face shows, and the tear has three points, not the single V of a banner tail.

1.0 equals mark. Round bends and a 30° spine, kept as a 1 in 2 diagonal on the 16 grid, so the strokes never stack into bars.
