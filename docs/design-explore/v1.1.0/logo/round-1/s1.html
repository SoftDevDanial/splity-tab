1MarkS1, abstract geometric, revision 2

S1, the fan

Three equal slips fanned from one pivot, a receipt dealt out into shares.

Construction

6 by 18 slips on the 28 px slot grid. The back two turn 27° about one pivot below the front foot, so each back foot lands on the 1 px gap line. Square corners only.

Pressure tests

  • Hand of cards. Card icons overlap round cornered 5 to 7 cards left to right. Here the corners are square, each strip shows its full 1 to 3 length, and the middle one covers both.
  • Paint swatch fan. Swatch books swing many strips on a rivet you can see. Here there are three, and the pivot is only implied.
  • Peacock. Round feathers fan half a circle around a body. Here the ends are square, the spread is 54°, and there is no body.
  • Loading icon. Spinner bars stand apart around an empty hub. Here both back strips run behind the front one, a stack, not a ring.
  • Letters. No D and no round side. At a glance it can still read as a W, which is the open risk.
