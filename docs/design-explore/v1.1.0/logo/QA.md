# Logo round 1, QA record, Splity Tab

Each sheet was rendered by the orchestrator in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 (console, network, overflow, the production mark alone at 16, 28 and 96 px), then reviewed by a separate `logo-qa` agent against the canon's rubric and the brief's hard constraints. Only a PASS reaches the board.

| Id | Idea | Verdict | Score | Weakest criterion | Risks for the owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | The fan | FAIL, first review, sent back | 22 of 28 | Composition truth 0 (one bar and two floating wedges, the pivot hidden, the slips unequal). Read as "V I V", a crest or a bat at 28 px | Revision asked, full-length strips behind the front one, a 1 unit gap |
| S1 | The fan, revision 2 | FAIL, RETIRED | 25 of 28 | Every fix verified, no gate at 0. Structural limit. Hidden back feet end in points and read as a W or crest, visible feet read as a stock three-card fan. No receipt cue can be added without a cliché, so it does not grow from the glass receipt as ruled | Not shown on the board. Replaced by S5 |
| S2 | The receipt S | FAIL, RETIRED | 23 of 28 | Distinctiveness 0. At the real slots (28 and 16 px) it is a plain bold S, the brief's category cliché, a font could set it. The tear is 1.46 px deep at 28 px and dropped at 16. Structural, a tear on a strip end can never pass 2 px at 28. Also the hidden sources drew below the sheet (3788 px tall) | Not shown on the board |

| S3 | The tear is the tick | **PASS**, first review | 26 of 28 | Concept and distinctiveness 1 each. At 28 px it reads as paper with a check (the proof lands, "receipt" and "tear" do not), close to a filled checkbox or a file-check icon. Clear of the bill-splitting category. Every carrying feature 2 px or more at 16 px, with no margin | Ceiling, paper plus a check is the document-check family. Polish not blocking, the 18 px row should use the 16 grid drawing, the sheet lacks its pressure-test notes |

**Lesson carried into the remaining briefs.** The idea must read at the real 28 px slot and in the 16 px favicon, with every feature that carries it at least 2 px. A feature that only reads at hero size is texture, not the idea.
