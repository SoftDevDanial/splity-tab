Recommended as core R28 and R32 (One person takes every spare sen, also an item's it did not have, At random walks the drawn order once across the bill to members of each division, eligibility is "on at least one item" in by-item and everyone in even, One person falls back to Top of the list while the person has nothing and the choice is kept).

- **CG-P4.** Which bill counts as "a new bill" that opens People on a phone. Recommended, any bill equal to `newBill()` (cold boot, Start new bill, an unreadable draft, or a reload of an untouched new bill), so the live gate's reload after Start new bill still shows `bill-empty`.
